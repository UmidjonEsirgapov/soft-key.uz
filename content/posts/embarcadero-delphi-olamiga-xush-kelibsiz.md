## Delphi dasturlash dunyosiga kirish

Ko'plab dasturlashga qiziquvchilar Delphi tilini o'rganishni qayerdan boshlash kerakligi haqida savollar berishadi. Delphi – bu hamon o'z dolzarbligini yo'qotmagan, yuqori unumdorlikka ega bo'lgan kuchli texnologiya. Ushbu maqola orqali siz Delphi olamiga kirib borishingiz va o'quv jarayonini tezlashtirishingiz uchun eng kerakli manbalarni topasiz.

### Nima uchun Delphi tanlash kerak?

Bugungi kunda Delphi jamoasi (community) juda faol. Dasturchilar uchun doimiy ravishda yangi kitoblar va video darsliklar tayyorlanmoqda. Masalan, Marco Cantuning [Object Pascal Handbook](https://github.com/MarcoDelphiBooks/ObjectPascalHandbook) asari tilning barcha zamonaviy xususiyatlarini chuqur o'rganish uchun ajoyib qo'llanma hisoblanadi. Shuningdek, Nick Hodgesning [Coding in Delphi](https://leanpub.com/codingindelphi) va [More Coding in Delphi](https://leanpub.com/morecodingindelphi) kitoblari professional darajadagi rivojlanishga yordam beradi.

### Professional rivojlanish uchun qo'shimcha resurslar

Agar siz tizim arxitekturasini tushunish va unumdorlikni oshirishni istasangiz, Primoz Gabriyelchichning [Delphi High Performance](https://www.packtpub.com/application-development/delphi-high-performance) kitobini o'qishni tavsiya qilaman. Bu yerda xotirani boshqarish, parallel dasturlash (PPL) va kodni optimallashtirish bo'yicha juda muhim ma'lumotlar mavjud. Shuningdek, [Parallel Programming with OmniThreadLibrary](https://leanpub.com/omnithreadlibrary) kutubxonasi yordamida qotmaydigan va tezkor ilovalar yozish imkoniyatiga ega bo'lasiz.

Delphi orqali nafaqat Windows, balki Linux, macOS, iOS va Android platformalari uchun ham dasturlar yaratish mumkin. Bu borada [Cross-Platform Development with Delphi 10.2 & FireMonkey for Windows, Mac OS X & Linux](https://www.amazon.com/Cross-Platform-Development-Delphi-FireMonkey-Windows/dp/1549545760) kitobi sizga yo'l-yo'riq ko'rsatadi.

### Amaliyot va hamjamiyat

Nazariy bilimlarni mustahkamlash uchun amaliyot shart. Siz [Delphi Demo dasturlar](https://sourceforge.net/p/radstudiodemos/code/HEAD/tree/branches/) orqali kodlar bilan tanishishingiz, [Alister Christie ning LearnDelphi.Tv](http://learndelphi.tv/) sayti yoki [Embarcaderoning 2 mingga yaqin videolar kutubhonasi](https://www.youtube.com/user/EmbarcaderoTechNet) orqali vizual tarzda o'rganishingiz mumkin. Bundan tashqari, [Delphi.uz](https://delphi.uz) va [Embarcadero Community](https://community.embarcadero.com) kabi platformalarda boshqa dasturchilar bilan fikr almashish imkoniga egasiz.

### Xulosa

Delphi – bu katta imkoniyatlar eshigi. Agar siz to'g'ri resurslardan foydalansangiz, qisqa vaqt ichida professional darajaga chiqishingiz mumkin. Yuqorida sanab o'tilgan kitoblar, [Pawel Glowacki tomonidan yozilgan maqolalar](https://community.embarcadero.com/blogs/entry/learn-to-program-with-starter) va [Begin..End](https://www.beginend.net/) kabi resurslar sizga dastlabki qadamlarda yaqindan yordam beradi. Savollaringiz bo'lsa, izohlarda yozib qoldirishingiz mumkin!
